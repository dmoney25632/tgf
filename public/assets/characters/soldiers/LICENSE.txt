Motion-captured soldiers: two sources, two licences.

1) Soldier meshes and textures (soldier_<a-h>.gltf / .bin / _1k.gltf,
   soldier_*_color*.jpg / .ktx2, soldier_*_normal.*, soldier_*_orm.*)

   Microsoft Rocketbox avatar library: Military_Male_01 (a), Military_Male_03 (b),
   Military_Male_04 (c), Military_Male_02 (d), Military_Male_05 (e),
   Military_Male_06 (f), Military_Female_01 (g), Military_Female_02 (h).
   https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox

   Changes: converted from FBX to glTF, scaled to metres, facial bones folded
   into the head, the knife removed, split into swappable parts (body, head,
   helmet, equipment) over one canonical joint order, each part given a
   simplified LOD, the four texture sets packed into one atlas per avatar
   (2048 / 1024 px colour, 1024 px normal), the specular map turned into a
   roughness channel plus team-tint and skin masks, beard / eye-band masks
   added to the heads, meshopt-compressed, KTX2 copies of the textures.
