First-person arms (fparms.gltf, fparms.bin, fparms_*.jpg / .ktx2)

   Microsoft Rocketbox avatar library: Military_Male_01.
   https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox

   Changes: the sleeves and gloved hands cut out of the body, smoothed and
   re-simplified to 16k triangles, re-rigged onto a two-arm first-person
   skeleton (forearm twist bones, three bones per finger), the textures
   cropped to the arms with a glove mask and per-region albedo normalisation.
   Built by scripts/fparms/build.mjs.
