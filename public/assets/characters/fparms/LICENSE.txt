First-person arms (fparms.gltf, fparms.bin, fparms_*.jpg / .ktx2)

   Based on the Microsoft Rocketbox avatar library: Military_Male_01.
   https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox

   Changes: the sleeves and gloved hands cut out of the body, smoothed and
   re-simplified, re-rigged onto a two-arm first-person skeleton (forearm
   twist bones, three bones per finger) by scripts/fparms/build.mjs; then
   rebuilt in Blender by art/blender/fparms (original work, same licence):
   the sleeve cut back to a pushed-up, turned hem and fitted to the forearm,
   a new glove cuff with a rolled rim and a hook-and-loop closure tab, bare
   wrist skin, a field watch on the left wrist, weights re-transferred and
   cleaned, new UV layout, and all textures (albedo, normal, roughness /
   metalness / region mask) replaced by procedurally painted ones: molded
   knuckle guard, finger pads, stitched seams, fourchettes, palm panels,
   ripstop sleeve with folds, skin, watch dial. No Rocketbox texture pixels
   remain; the mesh of the hands and sleeves derives from Rocketbox.
